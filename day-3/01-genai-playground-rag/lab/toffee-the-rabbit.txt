The Adventures of Toffee the Rabbit

Toffee is a five-year-old rabbit who lives in Waterford City. She has soft caramel-coloured fur that earned her the name Toffee when she was just a tiny kit, small enough to fit in the palm of a hand.

Toffee's absolute favourite foods are hay and bananas. Every morning she wakes up and heads straight for her hay rack, pulling out long strands and munching happily while watching the world go by from her window overlooking the River Suir. But the real highlight of her day comes in the afternoon when she gets a slice of banana — she does a little binky jump every single time, no matter how many times it happens.
